About the Framework

Purpose

This student project demonstrates defensive privacy engineering, risk modeling, security awareness, explainable scoring, and privacy-by-design application development.

What it does

  • Collects only voluntary assessment answers.
  • Calculates a transparent 0–100 exposure score.
  • Breaks risk into privacy/security categories.
  • Produces actionable recommendations and a PDF report.
  • Provides synthetic profiles for safe demonstrations.

What it does not do

  • No real social-media scraping.
  • No account enumeration.
  • No private-account access or privacy-setting bypass.
  • No tracking or profiling of real individuals.
  • No collection of passwords or private messages.
